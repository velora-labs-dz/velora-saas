# VELORA — MARKETPLACE

## Status

Future. Do not build before SaaS has real customer traction.

## 1. Strategic role

Velora SaaS gives businesses operational infrastructure.

Velora Marketplace eventually gives those businesses customer acquisition.

The long-term flywheel:

```text
More businesses
 → more services
 → more availability
 → more consumer choice
 → more bookings
 → more business revenue
 → more businesses
```

## 2. Consumer

Future capabilities:

- account
- location
- search
- filters
- business profiles
- services
- availability
- booking
- payment
- favorites
- reviews
- notifications

## 3. Business listing

Businesses eventually manage:

- public profile
- photos
- descriptions
- categories
- services
- availability
- pricing
- booking settings

## 4. Booking

A marketplace booking should create or reference a real SaaS appointment.

Do not maintain two separate appointment truths.

## 5. Reviews

A review should preferably reference a completed booking or another verified interaction.

## 6. Search

Potential ranking signals:

- distance
- availability
- price
- rating
- reliability
- popularity
- relevance

Begin with transparent simple ranking.

## 7. Marketplace and SaaS boundary

SaaS remains the authoritative source for:

- business
- service
- staff
- schedule
- appointment
- operational payment state

Marketplace is a discovery and transaction surface.

## 8. Future extension

Potential future products:

- consumer wallet
- loyalty
- offers
- promotions
- marketplace ads
- partner tools

None belong in Phase 1.
